    Field manual

    How to Play

    Objective

    Clear every rival from the arena; stages get harder, but your build carries over. Blasts and rival contact cost a life, a shield absorbs one, and the run ends when lives run out.

    Bombs

    Space drops a bomb. Blasts travel in a cross, stop at walls, destroy the first brick (unless the blast is piercing), and chain-detonate other bombs.

    Power-ups

    Bricks hide extra bombs, fire range, speed, and shields. Walk into one to collect it.

    Intermission

    After each stage, choose one of three upgrades with 1–3 or Tab + Enter. Upgrades last for the whole run.

    Techniques

    Run-long techniques. Bomb Kick: walk into a resting bomb to send it sliding. Remote Detonation: press X to trigger your live bombs early. Piercing Blast: blasts break every brick in range. Fuses still run.

    Daily Runs

    Daily Run seeds the arena from your local date, so every attempt today is the same challenge. Replay it all day; the Daily Best is your highest score for today, and daily runs still count toward your all-time bests.

    Records

    Records opens from the title or game over. It shows your all-time bests, today's Daily Best, and the 20 most recent runs with mode, date, score, stage, stats, and techniques.

    Controls

    Arrows or WASD move · Space bomb · X detonate · Esc or P pause · M mute
