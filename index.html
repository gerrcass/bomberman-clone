    Field manual

    How to Play

    Objective

    Clear every rival from the arena; stages get harder, but your build carries over. Blasts and rival contact cost a life, a shield absorbs one, and the run ends when lives run out.

    Bombs

    Space drops a bomb. Blasts travel in a cross, stop at walls, destroy the first brick, and chain-detonate other bombs.

    Power-ups

    Bricks hide extra bombs, fire range, speed, and shields. Walk into one to collect it.

    Intermission

    After each stage, choose one of three upgrades with 1–3 or Tab + Enter. Upgrades last for the whole run.

    Techniques

    Run-long techniques: Bomb Kick, Remote Detonation, Piercing Blast. Walk into a resting bomb to kick it; it slides until terrain, another bomb, or a bomber stops it. With Remote Detonation, press X to detonate every live bomb you own; fuses still run.

    Controls

    Arrows or WASD move · Space bomb · X detonate · Esc or P pause · M mute
